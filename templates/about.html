Our story

{{ doodle('heart', 'big') }}

Crumb & Petal is a small, imaginary shop on a quiet corner of Prague: one counter where you can pick up a warm croissant, a flat white and a bunch of tulips in the same visit.

It is also a course project. We invented the shop, its menu and its prices to practise building something real end to end: a website, an ordering form, and a chat assistant that only answers from the shop's own information and says so honestly when it doesn't know.

What we care about

  • Cosy and minimal. A calm place with good bread, good coffee and flowers that make a day better.
  • Honest food labels. Gluten-free and lactose-free items are clearly marked, and we're upfront that our kitchen also handles wheat, nuts and dairy.
  • Simple promises. Next-day flower delivery in Prague, card or cash, and a team that replies to every request by email.
